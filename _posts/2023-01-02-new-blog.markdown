It's been almost five years since I wrote anything here. There wasn't any special reason, just got busy with work, then school took over my life 

Coming back is a little strange. Some of the old posts read like they were written by someone else who was more confident than I remember feeling. I've thought about deleting them. I'm leaving them up.

I miss writing. It forces me to actually understand thing with real depth beyond the surface level. Neither work nor my classes quite fill that gap.
