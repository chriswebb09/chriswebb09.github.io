It's been almost five years since I wrote anything here. I'd love to say there was a good reason. There wasn't, really — work got busy, then school did, and the blog was always the easiest thing to put off, until it quietly became the thing I didn't do anymore.

Coming back is a little strange. Some of the old posts read like they were written by someone else — more confident than I remember feeling, and wrong in a few places I couldn't see at the time. I've thought about deleting them. I'm leaving them up.

The honest reason I'm writing again is that I miss it. Writing is the only thing that forces me to actually understand something instead of just nodding along at it, and neither work nor my classes quite fill that gap.

I'm not going to promise a schedule. That's roughly how I ended up with a five-year silence last time. But there are a few things I've been wanting to work through, and this is where I'll try to do it.
